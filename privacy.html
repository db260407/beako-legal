Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026. The German version is legally binding.

In short
  • Beako only stores what the app needs to work: your account, your goals and your progress.
  • No ads, no selling of data, no third-party advertising or analytics trackers.
  • The coach works without any AI service – your messages never leave our database.
  • Your data is stored on servers in Frankfurt (EU).
  • You can export all your data in the app and delete your account completely with one tap.

1. Controller

David Berger, [street address], [postcode and city], Germany. Email: [email address]

2. What we process and why

Account

Email address and password (stored only as a hash), or an identifier from Sign in with Apple. Purpose: providing your account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Profile

Display name, chosen profile picture, friend code, language, reminder time, app settings (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Goals, plans and progress

Your goals and “why”, the plans the app builds from them (calculated on your device by fixed rules – no automated decisions with legal effect, no profiling), check-ins, notes, weekly reviews, coins, titles, learning progress and quiz results (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Health-related data

Some entries may reveal information about your health – e.g. weight or nutrition goals and entries on the “dark side” (smoking, alcohol, sugar, snacks, screen time, daily amounts, panic-button entries). We only process these because you gave your explicit consent when signing up (Art. 9(2)(a) and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by deleting the goal or your account, or by emailing us.

Coach chat

Your messages and the coach’s answers are stored in your account so you can see the history. Answers are generated by the app itself from keywords – no AI service or third party is involved. The previous day’s history is deleted automatically; weekly reviews are kept.

Friends, chat, challenges and league

Friends see your display name, profile picture, title, your profile card (goal progress in percent, no notes or amounts), your coins of the current week in the league and your progress in shared challenges. Chat messages and photos are only visible to you and the other person. Chat photos are deleted automatically after 48 hours.

Blocking and reporting

You can block people and report content. For a report we store who reported whom, the reason and the related chat so we can review it (usually within 24 hours). Reports are deleted 12 months after they are closed. Messages that clearly break our rules may be blocked automatically when sent (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – a safe community).

Camera, photos and microphone

Only requested when you use a feature that needs it: camera for scanning a friend’s QR code and taking chat photos, photos for picking a chat photo, microphone in the speaking exercise to detect pauses. The voice recording stays on your device and is deleted right afterwards – it is never uploaded.

Reminders and push notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. For notifications from friends we store a push token of your device; delivery runs through Expo’s push service (650 Industries, Inc.) and the Apple Push Notification service. You can turn push off in iOS Settings at any time.

Purchases

Subscriptions are bought through Apple’s App Store. We never receive payment details – only a signed confirmation with product, transaction number and term, which we verify to unlock Premium.

Anonymous usage statistics (only with your consent)

If you agree, the app sends anonymous events such as “onboarding step reached” – not linked to your account, only with a random device identifier, no content. It runs on our own server, not with an analytics company (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). Withdraw any time under More → Privacy. Raw data is deleted after 14 months.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it sends a technical report (error message, code location, app version, device model, iOS version) – without your name, email or content. Deleted after 90 days (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3. Recipients and processors

  • Supabase, Inc. – database, file storage, sign-in (processor under Art. 28 GDPR); servers in Frankfurt (EU), EU Standard Contractual Clauses for possible US access.
  • Apple – App Store, purchases, Sign in with Apple, push delivery (EU-US Data Privacy Framework).
  • Expo (650 Industries, Inc.) – push notification forwarding (USA, Standard Contractual Clauses).

We do not share your data otherwise unless required by law. We never sell data or use it for advertising.

4. Retention

Account data as long as your account exists; chat photos 48 hours; coach history until the next day; reports 12 months after closing; crash reports 90 days; anonymous statistics 14 months. Deleting your account (More → Delete account) removes all your data, including chat photos, immediately.

5. Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR) and withdrawal of consent (Art. 7(3)). More → Privacy → Export my data creates a file with all your data. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

6. Security, age, emergencies

All connections are encrypted (TLS); database access rules ensure everyone only sees their own data and what is shared with friends. Beako is intended for people aged 16 and over. Beako is not a medical service – in an emergency call your local emergency number; crisis lines for your country: findahelpline.com.
